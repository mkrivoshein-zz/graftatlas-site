Hair transplant clinics in Bangkok

Thailand has one of the most established medical tourism industries in the world. Bangkok draws hair transplant patients from Asia-Pacific, Australia and the Middle East, typically at prices between Turkey and the West.

We found 39 hair transplant providers in Bangkok on Google Maps. Compared with Istanbul, a larger share are small doctor-led clinics, several run by surgeons who list ISHRS or ABHRS credentials, and some programmes operate inside private hospitals. Many listings serve mainly Thai patients, so English-language reviews can be scarce.

Always confirm which doctor will operate and what their credentials are. Our checklist of what to watch for explains what to ask.

How to read this table

  • Google rating and reviews are taken from Google Maps on 21 September 2026. We list every hair transplant provider we found; salons, wig shops and purely cosmetic clinics are excluded.
  • Type. “Agent / facilitator” means the company sells surgery performed in partner clinics or hospitals, often in another city or country. “Mostly non-surgical” and “Aesthetics / med spa” mean transplants are not their main service.
  • Founded (claimed) is what the clinic or business directories say. It is not verified.
  • Domain since is the registration year of the clinic's website domain (public WHOIS data). It is an independent hint at how long the business has been online; a recent date can also mean the domain was re-registered.
  • Inclusion is not an endorsement. GraftAtlas does not currently receive fees from any clinic listed here. Spotted an error? Write to hello@graftatlas.com.
