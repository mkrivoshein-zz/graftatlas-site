Cities we're covering

Why Istanbul

Istanbul is widely seen as the world capital of hair transplant tourism. Hundreds of clinics sell all-inclusive packages (surgery, hotel, transfers) at a fraction of US or UK prices, and patients fly in from Europe, the Middle East and beyond. The range in quality is just as wide, including clinics where technicians do most of the work. That is why we start here.

See all 60 providers in Istanbul →

Why Miami

Miami is one of the main hair restoration hubs in the US and a gateway for patients from Latin America. Surgery is done by US-licensed physicians under US rules, at prices several times higher than in Turkey. It gives us a benchmark for what the US standard of care costs.

See all 32 providers in Miami →

Why Bangkok

Thailand has one of the most established medical tourism industries in the world, with internationally accredited hospitals and specialised clinics. Bangkok draws hair transplant patients from Asia-Pacific, Australia and the Middle East, typically at prices between Turkey and the West.

See all 36 providers in Bangkok →

Why Budapest

Budapest built its reputation on dental tourism and now attracts hair transplant patients too. For Western Europeans it offers EU regulation, short flights and prices well below home, which makes it a common alternative to Istanbul.

See all 18 providers in Budapest →

Why Warsaw

Poland offers EU standards of care at lower prices than Western Europe, and Warsaw clinics see many patients from the UK, Germany and Scandinavia. It is a useful comparison point for people who want to stay inside the EU.

See all 26 providers in Warsaw →

Why Georgia

Georgia is a smaller, fast-growing hub with Turkish-style packages at similar prices. Tbilisi has some of the region's longest-running clinics and well-known surgeon training centres, and Batumi on the Black Sea is adding clinics aimed at Turkish and Middle Eastern patients.

See all 11 providers in Georgia →

Why Tijuana

Tijuana is Mexico's main hub for hair transplant tourism, right across the border from San Diego. Clinics are built around US patients, with English-speaking staff, airport or border pickup and prices well below US clinics. Mexico City and Guadalajara also have busy clinics, mostly serving local patients.

See all 21 providers in Tijuana →

Why Mexico City

Mexico City is Mexico's largest hair transplant market, with most patients local and a growing number flying in from the US. It mixes surgeon-led clinics with national chains that also sell non-surgical treatments. Prices are well below US clinics, and several clinics have English-speaking doctors.

See all 28 providers in Mexico City →

Other destinations

  • India (Delhi, Mumbai): some of the lowest prices anywhere and a large pool of experienced surgeons. Quality varies a lot from clinic to clinic.
  • South Korea (Seoul): known for skilled surgeons and non-shaven techniques, popular with patients across Asia.
