Hair transplant clinics in Sydney

Sydney is Australia's largest hair transplant market, and the most expensive one in our catalogue: patients often compare a local procedure with flying to Turkey. Clinics are split between surgical practices and chains that mainly sell non-surgical hair loss treatments.

We found 19 hair transplant providers in Sydney on Google Maps. Several are branches of the same national chain, and a number of clinics focus on PRP and PRF courses rather than surgery — their reviews are about treatments, not transplants. Australian domain records do not show registration dates, so the founded year comes from the clinics themselves.

Complaints most often mention upselling at the consultation, hairlines set lower than agreed and density below what was quoted. Ask who performs each step, get the graft count in writing, and check whether the price covers revision surgery. See our checklist of what to watch for.

How to read this table

  • Google rating and reviews are taken from Google Maps on 21 September 2026. We list every hair transplant provider we found; salons, wig shops and purely cosmetic clinics are excluded. Clinics without a website are not listed.
  • Type. “Agent / facilitator” means the company sells surgery performed in partner clinics or hospitals, often in another city or country. “Mostly non-surgical” and “Aesthetics / med spa” mean transplants are not their main service.
  • Founded is the earliest of two years: the founding year the clinic or business directories give (not verified) and the year the clinic's website domain was registered (public WHOIS data). A recent domain can also mean the site was simply re-registered. n/a means neither year is known.
  • Inclusion is not an endorsement. GraftAtlas does not currently receive fees from any clinic listed here. Spotted an error? Write to hello@graftatlas.com.
