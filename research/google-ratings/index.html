Google ratings barely separate hair transplant clinics

Published 16 November 2026. Google data collected 21-24 September 2026.

We listed 327 hair transplant providers in 12 cities, from Istanbul to Seoul, with their Google Maps rating and review count. Of the 320 that have a rating, 237 (74%) are rated 4.8 or higher. Only 5 are below 4.0. If you are choosing a clinic, the star rating removes almost nobody from your list.

Big clinics are not rated lower

Perfect 5.0 scores are mostly a small-clinic thing: half of the providers with fewer than 50 reviews have one. But busy clinics do not drift down. Of the 28 providers with 1,000 or more reviews, 24 (86%) are rated 4.8 or higher, a larger share than among small clinics.

Two more things the list shows

Some "clinics" are agencies. 17 of the 327 providers describe themselves as medical travel agencies or facilitators: they sell surgery that is done in someone else's clinic. Most are in Istanbul (8 of 60) and Warsaw (7 of 26). On Google Maps they look the same as clinics and are rated just as high.

Many websites are new. We looked up the registration year of 294 provider websites in public WHOIS records. The median is 2018. 126 (43%) were registered in 2020 or later, and 70 (24%) in 2023 or later. A new domain does not make a clinic bad, since clinics rebrand and move, but it is worth asking how long the team has actually been operating.

What to look at instead

The rating tells you that a clinic is not a disaster. To tell good clinics apart you need other questions: who operates, how many grafts in one session, what happens if the result is poor. The number of reviews and how long the clinic has existed help a little. We collected the questions we would ask in Before you book.

Method

Each city list is built by hand from Google Maps searches in English and the local language ("hair transplant", "hair restoration clinic", "trasplante capilar" and so on). We keep every provider that does hair, beard or eyebrow transplants and has a website, and we drop salons, wig shops and providers without an address. The type (clinic, agency, aesthetics) comes from the provider's own website and Google category. Ratings and review counts are what Google Maps showed on 21-24 September 2026; they change every day. Domain years come from public WHOIS records.
