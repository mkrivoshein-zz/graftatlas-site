Hair transplant clinics in Miami

Miami is one of the main hair restoration hubs in the US and a gateway for patients from Latin America. Surgery is done under US medical rules, typically at several times Turkish prices.

The market is much smaller than in Istanbul: we found 32 providers in the Miami metro area on Google Maps, including Fort Lauderdale and Aventura. It mixes solo surgeon practices, US chains with several locations, and, more recently, Turkish clinics opening US branches. Some listings are med spas or hair-system studios where transplant surgery is only a side service, and we mark them as such.

Review counts here are far lower than in Istanbul, so a single review carries more weight. Read our checklist of what to watch for before you commit.

How to read this table

  • Google rating and reviews are taken from Google Maps on 21 September 2026. We list every hair transplant provider we found; salons, wig shops and purely cosmetic clinics are excluded. Clinics without a website are not listed.
  • Type. “Agent / facilitator” means the company sells surgery performed in partner clinics or hospitals, often in another city or country. “Mostly non-surgical” and “Aesthetics / med spa” mean transplants are not their main service.
  • Founded is the earliest of two years: the founding year the clinic or business directories give (not verified) and the year the clinic's website domain was registered (public WHOIS data). A recent domain can also mean the site was simply re-registered. n/a means neither year is known.
  • Inclusion is not an endorsement. GraftAtlas does not currently receive fees from any clinic listed here. Spotted an error? Write to hello@graftatlas.com.
